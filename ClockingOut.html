Clocking Out

Time Scale: 5 Days
Group Size: 5 People
Languages: GDScript
Software: Godot
Personal Project​

Created for the Brackeys Game Jam 2025.1 - “Nothing can go wrong...”.

An eerie puzzle game, where you spend the last hours of your workday closing up the petrol station you work at, per the instructions your boss left behind for you. As long as you do as you're told, nothing can go wrong.

Contributions:

  • Worked as a programmer - focused on Audio, the puzzles and the dialogue feature, as well as bug fixing.
